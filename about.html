> THE STORY BEHIND SOUNDORA


soundora started with a simple love for music and that special feeling you get from vinyl records.


We see music as more than just sounds; it's tied to our memories, emotions, and experiences. That's why we aimed to build a space where music fans can find their favorite albums, explore new artists, and connect with music on a deeper level.
